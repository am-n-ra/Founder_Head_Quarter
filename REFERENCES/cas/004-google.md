# Google — Case Study

## Problem
Larry Page was frustrated that web search results were terrible. AltaVista, Excite, Yahoo all used keyword matching. The problem was personal: he needed better search for his research.

## Sacrifice
Both put their Stanford PhD programs on hold. Maxed out credit cards to buy hard drives ($15K). Turned down lucrative job offers.

## Demo
PageRank algorithm running on Stanford's infrastructure (1996-1997). Imperfect: the university network was constantly congested, interface was minimal.

## Distribution
Big-fish: initially thought about selling to Excite or Yahoo. When they refused, started Google.com as a service.

## Early Believer
Andy Bechtolsheim (Sun co-founder) wrote a $100,000 check after a demo at a Stanford event. The check was made out to "Google Inc." before the company existed.

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated September 1998 after Bechtolsheim's check forced them to create a legal entity.

## Inflexion
AOL partnership (1999-2000) and the ad-driven revenue model. Unpredictable: Overture (GoTo) had invented paid search but Google perfected it.
