# Suno — Case Study

## Problem
Mikey Shulman had a background in audio AI at Kensho and saw that music creation was inaccessible to most people. The problem was personal: he wanted to make music but lacked the skills and tools.

## Sacrifice
Left stable roles at Kensho (acquired by S&P Global) and Google. Self-funded early research before raising venture capital. Bet on a technically hard problem (music generation) when AI video was the hot category.

## Demo
Suno v1 (2023) generated short music clips from text prompts. Product was initially imperfect: distorted audio, limited duration, often off-key. But it was the first AI that could generate original music with vocals.

## Distribution
Open: launched via Discord and web app, free tier to build community. Integrated with Microsoft Copilot for massive distribution.

## Early Believer
The Discord community of music creators. Then Microsoft partnership (Copilot integration) brought millions of users.

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated 2022 before building the product.

## Inflexion
Suno v3 (2024). Music quality crossed the threshold from "interesting experiment" to "actually usable." Unpredictable: the quality jump came from data scaling, not new architecture.
