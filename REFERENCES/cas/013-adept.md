# Adept — Case Study

## Problem
David Luan experienced the problem at Google: AI models could understand language but couldn't use software tools. The problem was personal — he wanted AI that could automate the boring parts of computing.

## Sacrifice
Ashish Vaswani and Niki Parmar left Google, leaving behind the Transformer legacy they co-created. Massive academic and industry reputation at stake. Left a sure thing (Google) for a risky startup.

## Demo
ACT-1 (2022) showed an AI that could use web browsers, CRMs, and spreadsheets. The product was initially imperfect: slow, required significant compute, and could only handle simple workflows.

## Distribution
Big-fish: targeted enterprises for workflow automation.

## Early Believer
The team's reputation (Transformer co-authors) attracted $350M in funding before any real product. Positioning: "the team that invented the Transformer is building the next leap."

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated April 2022 after deciding to start.

## Inflexion
The fundraising moment — $350M at $1B+ valuation with no revenue. Unpredictable: the market suddenly valued "AI agents" as the next category.
