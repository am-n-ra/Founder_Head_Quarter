# Pika Labs — Case Study

## Problem
Demi Guo experienced the problem as a Stanford researcher: making videos required professional tools (Premiere Pro, After Effects) or hiring experts. The problem was personal — she wanted to create videos for research presentations.

## Sacrifice
Left Stanford PhD (both founders were at Stanford CS). Turned down offers from major AI labs. Bet on consumer AI video when others were building for enterprise.

## Demo
Released text-to-video generation via Discord (April 2023). Product was initially imperfect: short clips (3 seconds), low resolution, inconsistent results. But the immediacy and ease of use was the breakthrough.

## Distribution
Open: launched on Discord (free). Grew to 500K+ users in months through viral sharing. The "waiting list" model created scarcity and demand.

## Early Believer
The Discord community became the first believers. Eventually, venture investors (Lightspeed, etc.) followed the community.

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated 2023 after the Discord community validated demand.

## Inflexion
Pika 1.0 launch with improved quality. The improvement from "3-second blurry clips" to "usable video" was the tipping point. Unpredictable: nobody expected a student project to compete with OpenAI's Sora.
