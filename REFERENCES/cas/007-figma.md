# Figma — Case Study

## Problem
Dylan Field interned at Flipboard and experienced design tool pain. Photoshop and Sketch were single-player tools — no real-time collaboration, no version history. The problem was personal: designers wasted hours on file sharing.

## Sacrifice
Dropped out of Brown University. Turned down full-time offers from LinkedIn and Flipboard. Moved to San Francisco on thin savings.

## Demo
Built a WebGL-based design prototype in 2013 with Evan Wallace. The browser-native approach seemed impossible — everyone said "you can't build a design tool in the browser." Product was initially imperfect (slow, limited features).

## Distribution
Niche: started as invite-only beta targeting designers tired of Sketch. Launched for free to build community. The "waiting list" created demand.

## Early Believer
Greylock ($4M seed, 2013). Positioning: "Figma is Google Docs for design."

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated in 2012 after the Thiel Fellowship and Greylock commitment.

## Inflexion
Sketch's file limit drama (2017). When Sketch users hit file limits and lost work, thousands migrated to Figma overnight. Completely unpredictable — Figma didn't cause it.
