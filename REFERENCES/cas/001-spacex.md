# SpaceX — Case Study

## Problem
Elon Musk wanted to send a greenhouse to Mars. Discovered launch costs were prohibitive. The problem was personal: he wanted to go to Mars himself.

## Sacrifice
Invested $100M+ of his own PayPal proceeds into a company everyone thought would fail. After 3 launch failures, was down to last rocket and last dollar.

## Demo
Falcon 1 reaching orbit in 2008 (4th attempt). Imperfect (small payload, high vibration) — but it worked. Product was initially imperfect.

## Distribution
Big-fish: targeted NASA, US Air Force, satellite operators. Won NASA CRS contract after Demo.

## Early Believer
NASA after Demo. Positioning: "we can do what NASA does at 1/10 the cost."

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated in 2002 after deciding to build rockets.

## Inflexion
NASA CRS contract after 3 failures. Unpredictable: could have gone bankrupt.
