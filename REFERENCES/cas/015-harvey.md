# Harvey AI — Case Study

## Problem
Winston Weinberg experienced the pain of legal research daily as a lawyer. The problem was personal: finding relevant case law was slow and expensive.

## Sacrifice
Both founders left their law firm careers. No salary for 18 months. Gabe Perey had a family to support. Irreversible: returning to big law after a failed startup is difficult.

## Demo
Built a GPT-4-based legal research assistant. Demo was prepared specifically for O'Melveny partners, showing real use cases. Product was initially imperfect: hallucinated occasionally, limited to US law.

## Distribution
Big-fish: targeted top law firms (O'Melveny, then others). Enterprise sales from day one.

## Early Believer
O'Melveny & Myers became the first customer after a cold email. The positioning was strategic: "GPT-4 fine-tuned for legal — your competitors are already exploring this."

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated 2022 after deciding to build.

## Inflexion
The cold email to O'Melveny's managing partner was answered within 24 hours — led to a pilot, then a contract, then became the reference customer. Unpredictable: cold email to a top law firm usually fails.
