# Airbnb — Case Study

## Problem
Brian Chesky and Joe Gebbia couldn't afford rent. A design conference was coming to SF and hotels were sold out. The problem was personal: "how do we pay rent this month?"

## Sacrifice
Maxed out credit cards ($25,000+). Created and sold Obama O's and Cap'n McCain's cereal boxes during 2008 election to fund the company. No salary for over 3 years.

## Demo
Put three air mattresses in their living room, advertised "air bed and breakfast." The site was terrible (basic HTML, no payment system on first version). Product was initially imperfect.

## Distribution
Niche: started with design conferences (SXSW, DNC 2008). Then expanded city by city, event by event.

## Early Believer
Paul Graham (Y Combinator). They were rejected initially, then accepted on a second try. YC gave $20K and the network that changed everything.

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated in 2008 after they realized they needed a legal entity for payment processing.

## Inflexion
Y Combinator acceptance (2009). Before that, they were making $200/week from cereal sales. YC forced them to focus on the core problem (not cereal).
