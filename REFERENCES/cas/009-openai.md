# OpenAI — Case Study

## Problem
The founders believed AGI would be the most important technology ever created. The problem: no lab was focused on safe AGI development as a non-profit. The problem was personal for Ilya (who thought about it for years) and Greg (who left Stripe because AGI was more important).

## Sacrifice
Greg Brockman left his CTO role at Stripe (massive equity sacrifice). Researchers left academia and industry. Musk invested initially but later left. Reputation risk was enormous — AI was considered a hype cycle.

## Demo
GPT-1 (2017) showed language models could learn without supervision. GPT-2 (2019) was considered "too dangerous to release fully." GPT-3 (2020) was the first commercial product. Each iteration was imperfect but demonstrated progress.

## Distribution
Big-fish: API for enterprises (2020), then ChatGPT for consumers (2022). Enterprise first, consumer explosion later.

## Early Believer
Elon Musk ($100M initial commitment), then Reid Hoffman, Peter Thiel, YC. The positioning was counter-positioning: "the best AI lab that isn't Google or Facebook."

## Incorporation
Day-one: incorporated as a non-profit in December 2015. Later added a capped-profit structure (2019).

## Inflexion
ChatGPT launch (November 2022). The viral moment was completely unpredictable — previous demos (GPT-2, DALL-E) had buzz but nothing like ChatGPT.
