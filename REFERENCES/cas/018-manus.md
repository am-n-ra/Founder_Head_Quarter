# Manus — Case Study

## Problem
The problem was personal to developers who wanted AI that could execute tasks, not just generate text. Existing AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude) could reason but couldn't browse, code, and manipulate files autonomously.

## Sacrifice
Left Tencent AI lab (stable research position). Self-funded early development. Bet personal reputation on an unproven agent architecture.

## Demo
Manus demonstrated autonomous browser navigation, code execution, and file manipulation in a single agent. Product was initially imperfect: slow, sometimes unreliable, limited to simple tasks.

## Distribution
Open: released via web app and developer API. Grew through AI developer communities and GitHub.

## Early Believer
The AI developer community was the first believer. The positioning: "the AI agent that actually works."

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated 2024 after the prototype showed promise.

## Inflexion
Competition between agent platforms (OpenAI GPTs, Anthropic, Manus) forced rapid improvement. Unpredictable: the market for AI agents exploded in 2024.
