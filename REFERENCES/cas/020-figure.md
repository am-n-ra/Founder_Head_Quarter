# Figure AI — Case Study

## Problem
Brett Adcock identified labor shortages as a generational problem. The problem was personal: his previous companies automated human processes, and he saw the limits — some tasks require physical presence.

## Sacrifice
Left a successful fintech exit (Vettery/Adecco). Self-funded Figure with $30M from his own pocket ($10M at a time). Bet personal wealth on humanoid robots when many robotics startups had failed.

## Demo
Figure 01 walking and manipulating objects (2023). Product was initially imperfect: slow, limited dexterity, tethered to power source. But it was a humanoid robot that could walk and grasp.

## Distribution
Big-fish: targeted manufacturing, warehousing, and logistics companies. BMW pilot deal was the first reference.

## Early Believer
OpenAI partnership validated the approach (2024). Then BMW became a pilot customer. The positioning: "humanoid robots for the 21st century factory."

## Incorporation
Day-one: incorporated May 2022 with significant personal capital committed.

## Inflexion
OpenAI partnership + BMW deal (2024). The combination of AI (OpenAI) and hardware (Figure) crossed the credibility threshold. Unpredictable: humanoid robots were considered a dead category until Figure proved otherwise.
