# Cognition / Devin — Case Study

## Problem
The founders had all worked on AI and knew that LLMs could reason about code, but nobody had built an autonomous coding agent. The problem was personal: as engineers, they wanted AI to handle the boring parts of software development.

## Sacrifice
Left prestigious roles: Steven Hao was a top engineer at Scale AI, Scott Wu came from competitive programming background. Left stable careers for an unproven approach (AI agents, not just LLMs).

## Demo
Devin (March 2024) — a demo video showed the AI agent solving real Upwork tasks autonomously (full-stack development, debugging, deployment). Product was initially imperfect: slow, expensive, made mistakes. But it demonstrated the paradigm shift.

## Distribution
Big-fish: targeted enterprises first (financial services, tech companies) with access to full codebases.

## Early Believer
Peter Thiel / Founders Fund (seed round). Positioning: "AI that can replace junior engineers."

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated November 2023 after deciding to build, before the demo was ready.

## Inflexion
The demo video went viral (10M+ views on social media). Completely unpredictable — they released a technical demo expecting developer interest, not mainstream explosion.
