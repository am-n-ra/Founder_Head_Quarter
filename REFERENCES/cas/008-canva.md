# Canva — Case Study

## Problem
Melanie Perkins taught design at university. Students struggled with complex tools (Photoshop, InDesign). The problem was personal: she saw the frustration daily.

## Sacrifice
Spent 5 years building towards the idea. Started with Fusion Books (school yearbook platform) to validate the thesis. Pitched over 100 VCs — all rejected. Continued anyway.

## Demo
Fusion Books proved non-designers could design (2006-2011). Then built Canva's browser-based design tool with Cameron Adams. Product was initially imperfect (limited templates, basic features).

## Distribution
Big-fish: launched with 1 million+ templates, free tier, integrated with schools and enterprises.

## Early Believer
Lars Rasmussen (ex-Google Maps) joined as advisor after being impressed by the demo. Brought credibility that unlocked the funding round.

## Incorporation
Trigger-based: incorporated in 2012 when ready to raise funding.

## Inflexion
Lars Rasmussen's involvement unlocked Bill Tai and the VC round (2013). Before that, 100+ VCs said no. Unpredictable: a random connection at a kitesurfing event changed everything.
