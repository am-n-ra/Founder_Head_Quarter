# Inflection AI — Case Study

## Problem
Mustafa Suleyman experienced the problem at DeepMind: AI was getting smarter but less personal. The problem was personal — he wanted to build an AI that could be a conversation partner, not just a tool.

## Sacrifice
Mustafa left DeepMind, a company he co-founded. Karén Simonyan left a prestigious research role. Both left their legacy behind.

## Demo
Pi (May 2023) — an AI assistant focused on supportive conversation rather than productivity. Product was initially imperfect: narrower capabilities than GPT-4, focused on conversation not task completion.

## Distribution
Open: launched Pi as a web app and mobile app, free to use. Focused on consumer adoption before enterprise.

## Early Believer
Reid Hoffman co-founded the company. The positioning: "my co-founder invented DeepMind. Now he's building AI you can talk to." Hoffman's network opened every door.

## Incorporation
Day-one: incorporated immediately with a large capital base ($225M initial raise).

## Inflexion
The Microsoft/GPU compute deal in 2023 gave Inflection access to 1000s of H100s. Unpredictable: the compute arms race in AI meant access to GPUs became the moat.
